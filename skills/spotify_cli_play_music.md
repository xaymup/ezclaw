# Skill: spotify_cli_play_music

## Description
How to use Spotify CLI (/usr/bin/spotify) to search and play Marwan Pablo tracks

## Procedure
1. Ensure Spotify CLI is installed and accessible at /usr/bin/spotify
2. Authenticate with Spotify:
   - Run: spotify login
   - Follow the OAuth flow in your browser
3. Search for Marwan Pablo tracks:
   - Run: spotify search "Marwan Pablo"
   - Browse the results
4. Play a specific track:
   - Run: spotify play "Marwan Pablo - [Track Name]"
   - Or use: spotify play "Marwan Pablo" to play the artist
5. Control playback:
   - spotify pause
   - spotify next
   - spotify previous
   - spotify volume 50
6. Manage playlists:
   - spotify playlist create "My Marwan Pablo Mix"
   - spotify playlist add "Marwan Pablo" "My Marwan Pablo Mix"
7. Important notes:
   - Keep Spotify Desktop app running for full functionality
   - CLI requires active Spotify session
   - Some features may need Spotify app in background
